Privacy

Northlight does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Updated 13 October 2026.

Your pictures

Northlight edits pictures on your own device. Photos you import, your edits and your finished pictures stay on the device, or in the storage you choose yourself (for example your iCloud Drive, your Google Drive or a folder). The North Watch never receives them.

Photos access

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, Northlight asks for access to your Photos library only to import the pictures you choose and to save finished pictures into its own album. You can change this at any time in Settings.

On-device features

Subject and people masks are computed on your device by Apple's Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded.

Optional AI services

If a future version offers cloud AI features, they will only run when you start them, with the provider named in the app, and only the picture you send is shared with that provider.
